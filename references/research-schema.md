# Research and evidence schema

Use these structures when the request requires real research rather than a short conceptual answer.

## Source hierarchy

Prefer evidence in this order, adjusting for the question:

1. Actual product behavior, official product material, original social posts, original comments, first-party platform guidance.
2. User reviews, forums, Reddit, interviews, support conversations, community discussions.
3. Government, regulators, standards bodies, research papers, reputable industry datasets.
4. High-quality reporting and specialist analysis.
5. Aggregators, creator lists, marketing blogs, AI-generated summaries.

Use lower levels to find leads. Do not let them carry high-stakes claims without corroboration.

## Case record

Capture one row per useful case:

| Field | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Case ID | Stable local identifier |
| Platform | TikTok, Instagram, Reddit, etc. |
| Market/language | Audience context |
| Creator/product | Source identity |
| Direct or analogous | Benchmark relationship |
| Content/title | Human-readable label |
| URL | Direct source link |
| Observed date | Date metrics and content were inspected |
| Public signals | Views, likes, comments, shares, saves, followers; preserve units |
| Hook | First-frame or first-line promise |
| Tension | Unresolved question or desired progress |
| Proof | Visible evidence or payoff |
| Comment psychology | Identity, correction, story projection, debate, etc. |
| Follow reason | Why a user expects future value |
| Repeatable mechanism | Abstract narrative structure |
| Production inputs | People, location, props, access, editing |
| Risk | Brand, cultural, legal, privacy, credibility |
| Target translation | Proposed use for the product or creator |
| Evidence/inference | Explicit label |
| Confidence | High, medium, low |

## User-language record

Capture comments, reviews, or interview observations as:

| Field | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Segment | Who is speaking |
| Situation | What they were trying to do |
| Desired progress | What better outcome they want |
| Anxiety | What could go wrong |
| Objection | Why they resist the product/content |
| Current workaround | What they do now |
| Trigger | What makes them seek a change |
| Exact phrase | Short compliant quote or close paraphrase |
| Source URL | Direct source |
| Frequency signal | One-off, recurring, or dominant |
| Interpretation | Hypothesis, clearly labeled |

Do not confuse comment volume with market prevalence. Comments reveal language and tensions, not automatically population size.

## Case scoring

Score 1–5 on:

- audience relevance;
- strength of observed signal;
- clarity of mechanism;
- ease of production;
- differentiation;
- follow potential;
- source reliability;
- compliance safety.

Record fatal constraints separately. A high total score never cancels a privacy, legal, safety, or credibility veto.

Recommended decision bands:

- **Adopt:** strong mechanism, feasible, safe, and strategically distinctive.
- **Adapt:** useful mechanism but needs a different proof, participant, tone, or platform package.
- **Watch:** suggestive evidence but insufficient or unstable.
- **Reject:** weak transfer, excessive cost, or unacceptable risk.

## Evidence sufficiency

A conclusion is normally ready for action when it has:

- one primary behavioral example plus corroborating user language; or
- several independent user examples showing the same tension; or
- authoritative factual support plus a content mechanism already demonstrated elsewhere.

Use a cheap experiment when evidence remains ambiguous and the downside is low. Research further when the decision is expensive, irreversible, regulated, or reputation-sensitive.

## Research stop rule

Stop collecting when all are true:

- new cases repeat known mechanisms;
- the next decision is clear;
- remaining uncertainty can be tested more cheaply than researched;
- enough source diversity exists to avoid one-community bias;
- production and measurement can be specified.

Do not chase completeness after decision value has saturated.
