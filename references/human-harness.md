# Human Harness

Use human judgment where strategy depends on commitment, taste, responsibility, consent, or execution reality. The human is a judgment layer, not merely a login helper or final recipient.

## Four checkpoints

### Decision checkpoint

Confirm:

- the exact decision the research can change;
- which findings would not change any action;
- non-negotiable constraints;
- decision owner and risk level;
- the cheapest acceptable path to evidence.

If the user has already supplied these, record them and continue. Ask only when materially different answers would redirect the work.

### Taste checkpoint

Review shortlisted content or positioning options:

- Is the case genuinely good, or only numerically large?
- Does value come from a transferable mechanism or an irreplaceable creator identity?
- Would repeated use make this a brand the team wants to become?
- Does the mode fit the desired trust, dignity, editorial voice, and audience relationship?
- Which option should be rejected even if it might earn short-term attention?

Do not pretend taste is an objective score. Preserve the reason and owner of the judgment.

### Contradiction checkpoint

Before a material commitment, surface:

- the evidence most likely to be misread;
- the conclusion that most conveniently confirms the starting preference;
- strongest disconfirming evidence and alternative explanation;
- retrieval, language, access, or sample limitations;
- the finding that would reverse the current decision.

Do not ask the human to approve a conclusion without showing the best case against it.

### Reality checkpoint

Before delivery, verify:

- the team can start the next action within the stated time horizon;
- required people, permissions, assets, engineering work, budget, and approvals exist;
- the first experiment has an owner, date, measurement event, and stop/continue rule;
- execution does not require another strategy meeting to interpret the plan;
- sensitive claims, filming, data, or user outcomes have the right consent and review.

## When to pause

Pause for the human only when:

- a choice changes brand identity or makes a costly commitment;
- taste determines which valid mechanism is acceptable;
- contradictory evidence supports materially different strategies;
- credentials, login, payment, legal approval, consent, or sensitive access is required;
- the proposed action exceeds the stated authority or constraints.

Otherwise make a reversible provisional judgment, label it, and continue. Human Harness must not become a blanket approval queue.

## Record format

Use `templates/human-checkpoint.md`. Every checkpoint records the options, agent recommendation, strongest objection, human decision or provisional assumption, owner, date, and downstream effect.
