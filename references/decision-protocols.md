# Decision and AI Value Protocols

Use this reference before a costly, multi-branch, reputation-sensitive, or hard-to-reverse research program. Its purpose is to prevent an agent from executing the wrong direction perfectly.

## Classify decision risk

| Level | Typical decision | Required standard |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Reversible | One post, one creative variant, one small audience test | Direct evidence or a clear hypothesis; run the cheap test quickly |
| Costly but reversible | Monthly content system, creator contract, paid campaign, positioning migration | Multiple source roles, disconfirming evidence, explicit trade-offs, human decision and reality checkpoints |
| Hard to reverse / reputation-sensitive | Cultural framing, legal/privacy claim, brand identity, large launch commitment | Primary or authoritative verification, contradiction and belief audits, named human owner, approval and reversal plan |

Do not demand enterprise-grade proof for a cheap reversible test. Do not use a reversible experiment to bypass consent, law, privacy, editorial integrity, or irreversible reputation risk.

## AI Value Gate

Before opening AI or multi-agent branches, answer:

```text
Uncertainty:
Decision it can change:
Can one primary source resolve it?
Can one real user or domain expert resolve it?
Does internal product/performance data own the answer?
Is a cheap reversible experiment faster?
Can a provisional judgment safely move the work forward?
What unique value would AI add?
Gate result: no AI / one bounded branch / multiple distinct branches
```

Valid AI contributions are new source access, synthesis, contradiction, multilingual coverage, structured extraction, or useful scale. “It can generate a longer answer” is not value.

Skip or shrink AI research when:

- the conclusion cannot change action;
- direct inspection is faster;
- internal data owns the truth;
- a fair experiment costs less than more research;
- the question depends mainly on taste, responsibility, or consent;
- the available AI cannot expose traceable sources.

## Mandatory Pre-mortem Belief Gate

Run this **before** every first-order strategic commitment: P0/primary channel, ICP, positioning wedge, launch narrative, comparison set, material budget allocation, brand-defining mode, costly production system, or cultural/privacy/legal claim. An unaudited belief cannot support a final recommendation.

```text
Working belief:
Why we want it to be true:
Strongest supporting evidence:
Strongest disconfirming evidence:
Alternative explanation:
Retrieval or sampling limit:
Evidence that would reverse the decision:
What remains true or useful if the belief is false:
Decision: retain / narrow / test / reject
Gate: PASS / PROVISIONAL-TEST / BLOCKED
```

- **PASS** requires supporting evidence, the strongest disconfirming evidence, an alternative explanation, retrieval/access/language limits, a reversal condition, and the residual decision.
- **PROVISIONAL-TEST** is allowed only for a reversible, bounded experiment. It cannot be described as validated strategy.
- **BLOCKED** prevents the commitment until evidence, scope, or user direction changes.

Audit both the conclusion and the path that produced it:

- Was the research question framed to invite confirmation?
- Did source selection favor one platform, language, customer type, or narrative?
- Was high engagement mistaken for strategic fit?
- Did inaccessible or conflicting evidence disappear from the summary?
- Does “no counterevidence found” reflect weak retrieval?
- Is the team protecting prior effort, identity, convenience, or a preferred creative style?

## Convergence and stop rule

Stop research when the next decision is clear and remaining uncertainty is cheaper to test. At convergence, preserve:

- evidence included and excluded, with reasons;
- strongest alternative explanation;
- unresolved uncertainty;
- reversal condition;
- selected and rejected directions;
- next experiment and observation window;
- human judgment still required.

Do not hide disagreement by averaging conflicting sources. Decide whether the conflict changes the decision, narrows the claim, or becomes an experiment variable.

When two or more AI services were used, complete [../templates/multi-ai-convergence.md](../templates/multi-ai-convergence.md) first. Deduplicate shared sources: four systems repeating one URL are one evidence path, not four votes. Unverified agreement remains a lead.
